Privacy, by design

Your health notes deserve a quiet place.

This prototype runs on your computer. It does not send your account or notes to a hosted service.

Local to this computer

The development server binds to loopback. Other devices cannot sign in to this prototype.

Passwords are hashed

The local service stores a salted scrypt hash, not your readable password.

Recovery stays with you

One-time recovery codes are shown once. Only their hashes are kept by the local service.
