02 / Write

Brand voice

One page to keep interface copy steady across routine care and high-stress moments.

Knowledgeable enough to trust. Human enough to live with.

Fieldnote Care helps families notice patterns, keep useful health details together, and know what to do next. We speak with calm confidence, respect the messiness of real life, and never pretend an app can replace a clinician. Care is practical and personal here: a shared record, a small next step, a clear handoff when more help is needed.

In the product

“A new note can help your care team see what has changed.”

Warm, never precious

Use plain, thoughtful language. Avoid baby talk, wellness clichés, and forced cheer.

Clear before clever

Lead with the action or fact. Save metaphor for quiet editorial moments, not instructions.

Support, don't diagnose

Describe what is recorded. Encourage a clinician's advice; never infer a condition.

Respect every family

Use chosen names, inclusive caregiver language, and neutral assumptions about food and bodies.

Small steps, no guilt

Invite a useful next action. Missing a log is a blank space, not a failure or broken streak.

Urgency without alarm

In SOS, use short verbs, visible choices, and local emergency guidance. No cute copy.

Do

“Add what you noticed.”
“Share this note with Maya's clinician.”

Don't

“Oops, you forgot!”
“Your symptoms mean you have…”

05 / Make

Icon & illustration

One coherent visual language, with a separate and unmistakable convention for urgent help.

Icons / Lucide outline

Use Lucide's familiar outline family: 24 px grid, 1.75 px stroke, rounded joins, no mixed filled / outline styles. Pair navigation icons with labels on primary destinations; icon-only controls need an accessible name and tooltip. Keep the SOS call action explicit and text-led.

Sketches are conceptual glyphs; use the Lucide package's official icons in the product.

Illustration / botanical field notes

Commissioned spot drawings with a loose pen-and-wash edge: leaves, simple body-neutral objects, a cup, a bedside lamp, a folded note. Two inks plus one muted wash. Mostly open space, slight hand-made irregularity, no cute faces, floating blobs, or anatomical shorthand that could imply diagnosis.

  • Use on landing, empty, and gentle onboarding states.
  • Keep records, charts, and urgent flows literal and unillustrated.
  • Prefer one clear spot image to decorative backgrounds; illustrations never carry instructions alone.

Direction proposed for approval

Quiet editorial care, paper / ink / fern palette with lake and citrus support colors, coral reserved for SOS, DM Serif Display + DM Sans, 4 px spacing, restrained 4 / 8 / 16 px radii, and botanical field-note illustration. Approve or call out changes here before screen design starts.
