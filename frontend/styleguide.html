Fieldnote Care / Working title

A quieter kind of clarity.

A reusable interface kit for shared family care. Paper-warm surfaces, precise controls, and human language make routine care easier to keep close.

Version 0.1 / 30 Sep 2026 Direction 01 / Editorial care WCAG-first states

Foundation / 10

Icon library

60 Lucide-style outline symbols, 24 px viewBox, 1.7 px stroke, currentColor. Use the sprite as <use href="icons.svg#name">.

Behavior / 11

State contract

State meanings stay consistent across the kit. A dash means the state has no useful meaning for that component; do not add decoration to imply one.
